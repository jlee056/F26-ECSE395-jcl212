## Team Contributions

**September 26, 2026 - Concept review with Mr. Levacy**
Ethan hosted the Zoom at 2 PM, and we presented our three concepts: a seat touch pad (A), a motion sensor (B), and a hinge angle sensor with a timer (C). George took notes in our Stakeholder Feedback document. Mr. Levacy liked that Concept C reliably catches standing use and is easy to clean, and he confirmed about five uses a day and no wall outlet near the toilet. He was worried that cleaning the toilet or the sensors could trigger Concepts A and B, and that a sensor watching the bathroom could make people uncomfortable. He did not like using an average bathroom time to decide a use is over, so we suggested a Done button.

**September 28, 2026 - Concept selection**
Based on his feedback, the team chose Concept C with the Done button. George finished the Stakeholder Feedback and Concept Selection write-up in the shared doc.
