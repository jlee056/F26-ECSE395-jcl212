**November 19, 2026 - Think[Box] laser cutting**

I went to Think[Box] and we laser cut the base out of wood to replace the cardboard base in our prototype. The cardboard version bent and wasn't very stiff, so the wood base should hold up better when we test the lid mechanism. We haven't mounted it on the prototype yet.

**Control group data**

I collected the control data on how often people close the toilet lid after a flush. We tallied it for two days at the two toilets in our house (3 residents upstairs, 2 downstairs). Over the two days there were 31 uses, 18 upstairs and 13 downstairs, and nobody closed the lid after flushing, so 0 of 31. This is the baseline our device will be compared against.
